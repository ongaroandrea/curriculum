LAURA

A robot that drives itself from A to B through a forest and recognises plants on the way. We won Challenge@PoliTo with a live demo outdoors.

Role
Team lead, 5 members
Context
Challenge@PoliTo, first place
Stack
ROS 2, YOLOv8, PyTorch, TD3, Gazebo, Python

Overview

I led a team of five. My part was plant species recognition with YOLOv8 inside ROS 2, and connecting a TD3 reinforcement-learning policy for obstacle avoidance to the LiDAR and GNSS planner.

We trained and tested in a Gazebo forest before taking the robot outside. The code is under NDA.

Results

First place at Challenge@PoliTo. In the live demo outdoors, the robot drove from A to B on its own.
